# 5-pin Single Row Female Headers - 0.1" (2.54mm)

## Description

Standard 1x5 (5-pin) single-row female headers with 2.54mm (0.1") pitch. Useful where four signals plus power or ground are required, or for compact multi-signal interconnects on PCBs and breadboards.

## Specifications

- Pitch: 2.54mm (0.1")
- Pin count: 5 (single row)
- Mounting: Through-hole
- Typical current rating: ~3A per pin (varies by vendor)
- Insulator: Black thermoplastic (typical)
- Contacts: Brass, often gold-flashed (varies by vendor)
- Operating temperature: -40°C to +85°C (typical)

## Common Uses

- Compact multi-signal connectors
- I/O expansion headers
- Sensor and module connections
- PCB and breadboard interconnects

- Generic vendor packaging (Dahszhi listing). Electrical ratings vary by manufacturer
