# MGI SpeedWare 1/4" NPT Cable Gland — Strain Relief Cord Grip

## Description

Nylon strain relief cable glands with 1/4" NPT threading for securing and protecting cables entering enclosures, junction boxes, and panels. The dome nut grips the cable mechanically when tightened, preventing pull-out and protecting the entry point from dust and moisture.

Rated IP68 — suitable for continuous submersion, and resistant to salt water, weak acids, alcohol, oil, grease, and common solvents. The 1/4" NPT size handles cables from 3mm to 6mm in diameter, making it ideal for typical power cords, sensor cables, and light-duty wiring into project enclosures.

## Specifications

### Physical Characteristics

- **Thread Size**: 1/4" NPT (National Pipe Thread)
- **Thread Pitch**: 14 TPI, 60° angle, 1.79° taper
- **Outer Diameter**: 0.54" (13.7mm)
- **Cable Diameter Range**: 0.10"–0.25" (3–6mm)
- **Material**: Nylon (black)
- **IP Rating**: IP68 (submersion-rated)

### Key Features

- IP68 rated — waterproof and dustproof
- Chemical resistant: salt water, weak acid, alcohol, oil, grease, common solvents
- Ergonomic gripping grooves on dome nut — can be hand-tightened or wrench-tightened
- Standard 1/4" NPT thread — fits common enclosure knockouts and threaded fittings
- No tools required to pass cable through and secure

## Installation Notes

1. Thread the gland body into the enclosure knockout (1/4" NPT threaded hole or use a lock nut on a plain hole)
2. Pass the cable through the gland from outside
3. Hand-tighten the dome nut — the internal grip ring compresses around the cable
4. Wrench-tighten for final seal if IP68 rating is required

## Applications

- Cable entry into 3D-printed or off-the-shelf project enclosures
- Junction box cable strain relief
- Panel-mount cable protection
- Outdoor or wet-environment wiring
- Sensor cable entry into weatherproof housings
- Power cord entry for custom power supplies

## Technical Notes

- 1/4" NPT is a tapered thread — apply PTFE thread tape for a watertight seal in the enclosure wall
- Fits cables 3–6mm OD — check cable jacket diameter before use (most 22–18 AWG multi-conductor cables fall in this range)
- For larger cables (6–12mm), use 3/8" or 1/2" NPT variants
- The gland grips the cable jacket, not the conductors — leave enough jacket inside the enclosure for proper strain relief
