# Tayda A-5479 - Black Knob, White Top, 15x15mm, Split Shaft

## Description

Black ABS potentiometer knob with white top cap, 15mm diameter x 15mm height, for 6mm T18 spline/split-shaft potentiometers. Part of the colored-top split-shaft knob family — the white top provides a clean, high-contrast look. The green-top counterpart (A-5480) is in Section A of the same bin.

Pairs with the 100K linear pot Tayda A-6333 (Cabinet 3, Bin 7) and other 6mm split-shaft pots.

## Specifications

### Physical Characteristics

- **Body Color**: Black
- **Top Color**: White
- **Material**: ABS
- **Diameter (bottom)**: 15mm
- **Height**: 15mm
- **Shaft Type**: T18 Split Shaft (spline)
- **Shaft Hole**: 6mm x 18T
- **Mounting**: Push-fit

### Key Features

- High-contrast white top for easy visual position reading
- ABS construction — durable and lightweight
- Standard 15x15mm form factor
- Fits any 6mm T18 split-shaft pot or switch
