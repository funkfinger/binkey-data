# Tayda A-5480 - Black Knob, Green Top, 15x15mm, Split Shaft

## Description

Black ABS potentiometer knob with green top cap, 15mm diameter x 15mm height, for 6mm T18 spline/split-shaft potentiometers. The contrasting green top makes it easy to see knob position at a glance. Part of the colored-top split-shaft knob family — matches the White Top (A-5479) and other color variants.

Pairs with the 100K linear pot Tayda A-6333 (Cabinet 3, Bin 7) and other 6mm split-shaft pots.

## Specifications

### Physical Characteristics

- **Body Color**: Black
- **Top Color**: Green
- **Material**: ABS
- **Diameter (bottom)**: 15mm
- **Height**: 15mm
- **Shaft Type**: T18 Split Shaft (spline)
- **Shaft Hole**: 6mm x 18T
- **Mounting**: Push-fit

### Key Features

- High-contrast green top for easy visual position reading
- ABS construction — durable and lightweight
- Standard 15x15mm form factor
- Fits any 6mm T18 split-shaft pot or switch
