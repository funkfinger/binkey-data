# Tayda A-6071 - Black Knob, White Indicator, 16x15mm

## Description

Classic black ABS potentiometer knob with a white indicator line, 16mm diameter x 15mm height. The clean black-and-white design is a staple of audio equipment and synthesizer panels. Fits any 6mm T18 split-shaft potentiometer.

A versatile, understated knob that looks professional on both DIY and commercial panels. The white indicator line provides clear position feedback without the bold colors of the pointer-style knobs.

## Specifications

### Physical Characteristics

- **Color**: Black with white indicator line
- **Material**: ABS
- **Diameter (bottom)**: 16.3mm
- **Height**: 14.4mm
- **Shaft Type**: T18 Split Shaft (spline)
- **Shaft Hole**: 6mm x 18T
- **Mounting**: Push-fit

### Key Features

- Classic black with white indicator line
- Clean professional appearance
- Standard 16x15mm size
- Fits any 6mm T18 split-shaft pot or rotary switch
